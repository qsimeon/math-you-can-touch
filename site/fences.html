    Keyboard and exact counting

    Click the board first. Arrow keys choose a fence, [ and ] slide it, Q and E turn it, and Z undoes.

    Each move is rounded to a whole-number line equation and counted exactly. The board refuses a move that would put two fences on the same line. Panning and zooming change only the view.

      Saved drawings

      The 93-yard arrangement reproduces Johannes Bader’s published construction. We rebuilt it with exact whole-number coordinates from Pavlo Savchuk’s LineOrder drawing. LineOrder is CC BY 4.0, and this is a modified reconstruction. The 76-yard drawing came from our own search. Neither is a new record.

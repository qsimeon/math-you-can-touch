      The important distinction

      Evidence answers a precise question.

      01

      Witness

      An exact arrangement with its validated faces shows that at least that many faces are possible. That is a lower bound for the stated model.

      02

      Proof

      A proof connects definitions to a conclusion. A formal proof assistant can check a specified theorem, but no such result is claimed here unless the supplied record says so.

      03

      Novelty

      Finding an arrangement or a trace does not establish that it is new. Novelty requires comparison with the relevant public record.

      Busy Beaver lesson

      Halting after a saved trace proves one lower bound.

      A machine that halts after a recorded number of steps demonstrates that this many steps are achievable. A timeout only says that this run did not halt before the cap. It does not prove an upper bound or non-halting.
